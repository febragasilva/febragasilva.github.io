<div class="about-content">
  <div class="about-section">
    <p class="about-text">
      河南大学基础医学院副教授，河南大学深圳研究院病原和媒介生物创新研究中心主任。2017年南方医科大学公共卫生学院预防医学专业本科，2023年南方医科大学病原生物学专业博士，南方医科大学公共卫生学院军事预防医学博士后。
    </p>
    <p class="about-text">
      主要研究方向：蚊媒病毒流行与进化、伊蚊野外生态行为和病毒蚊虫感染机制。
    </p>
    <p class="about-text">
      提出了可比较和可追踪的全球登革病毒统一基因分型框架，发现登革病毒洲际-基因型、区域-基因亚型和国家-基因分支对应层面展的地理限制性特征，构建了GISDD分型平台网站。于Interdisciplinary Medicine、iMetaOmics、Infectious Diseases of Poverty等期刊发表多篇SCI论文，申请/授权发明专利4项，获授权实用新型专利3项。参与起草国家标准1项，以主要起草人撰写团体标准2项。
    </p>
    <p class="about-text">
      iMeta、Interdisciplinary Medicine、Decoding Infection and Transmission等学术期刊青年编委，BMC Infectious Diseases学术期刊编辑，STAR Protocols等学术期刊审稿人。
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="about-section">
    <p class="about-text">
      Bachelor's degree in Preventive Medicine from the School of Public Health, Southern Medical University (2017); Ph.D. in Pathogenic Biology from Southern Medical University (2023); Postdoctoral researcher in Military Preventive Medicine at the School of Public Health, Southern Medical University.
    </p>
    <p class="about-text">
      Primary research focuses on the epidemiology and evolutionary dynamics of mosquito-borne viruses, field ecological behaviors of Aedes mosquitoes, and molecular mechanisms underlying viral infection in mosquito vectors.
    </p>
    <p class="about-text">
      Developed a globally standardized and traceable genotyping framework for dengue virus, revealing geographically restricted patterns across intercontinental-genotype, regional-genosubtype, and national-genobranch levels. Led the development of the GISDD genotyping platform, an accessible web-based tool for dengue virus classification.
    </p>
  </div>
